Ledger

The rules, signed and chained.

All control state lives in an append-only, hash-chained ledger on Walrus. If it can't be shown complete and intact, Steward can't pay anyone.

Records

Each record names its predecessor's hash. A gap, fork, wrong signer or rollback blocks every payment.

Payees

The only addresses Steward can pay, each added by Ada with a typed confirmation.
