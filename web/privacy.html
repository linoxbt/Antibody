In short

  • No accounts, no email, no tracking. The site sets no analytics or advertising cookies.
  • The sandbox is simulated. No real coins, keys or Walrus storage are involved, and it never asks for a transaction you would pay for.
  • Your own install stays on your computer. We never see its keys, memories or payments.

Hosted sandbox

When you sign in to the sandbox, your wallet signs a one-time text message. That proves you control the address; it cannot move funds. We keep:

  • Your wallet address, as the name of your sandbox and its simulated owner.
  • Your sandbox: simulated lane keys, ledger, payees and memories, all generated by the server. Anything you type into it (payee labels, instructions to Steward) is stored there.
  • A session in an HttpOnly cookie for 12 hours. The server stores only a hash of it.
  • Request logs kept by our hosting providers (Netlify, Railway), which include IP addresses, for their usual retention.
  • Counts (sign-ins, requests, errors) with no addresses or IPs in them.

Sandboxes nobody opens for 14 days are deleted automatically. You can delete yours at any time: Settings → Reset sandbox replaces it with a fresh one. It may also be cleared during maintenance.

Don't put real personal or financial information in the sandbox. It is a demonstration.

Your own install

Antibody installed with the installer runs entirely on your machine and talks only to the services you configure: the Walrus Memory relayer, a Sui RPC (Tatum by default), the Tatum Data and Storage APIs, an LLM provider if you set one, and alert channels if you set them. Their own privacy terms apply to what you send them. Nothing goes to us.

Incident reports you publish with antibody report are uploaded to Walrus through Tatum and are public by design. They contain the quarantined memories and the ledger record that quarantined them.

This website

Pages are static files served by Netlify. Your theme choice and whether you entered the recorded demo are kept in your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere. Fonts are self-hosted.
